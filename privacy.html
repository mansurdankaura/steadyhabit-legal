Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

The short version: Steady Habit has no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don’t collect, receive, sell or share your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

1. What the app stores — on your device only

  • Optional account – if you create one: your email address, an optional display name, and your password (kept in the iOS Keychain). The account exists only on your device; it is not sent anywhere.
  • What you log – the habits you track, daily check-ins (mood, urges, notes, slips), urge events, triggers, journal entries, missions and achievements.
  • Emergency contacts – names and phone numbers you type in yourself. The app does not read your address book.
  • Settings – such as reminder times, sounds and theme.

2. What we don’t collect

Nothing leaves your device through us. The app makes no network requests to us or any third party, and includes no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. We don’t access your location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera or health data.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications – if you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Apple Watch – if you use the Watch app, your iPhone and Watch exchange data directly using Apple’s WatchConnectivity. It does not pass through us.

4. Backups and sharing

Encrypted backups are protected with AES-GCM using a password you choose, and saved wherever you pick (for example Files or iCloud Drive). If you save to iCloud Drive or share a summary, report or backup through the iOS share sheet, Apple’s or the receiving service’s privacy terms apply to that copy. We never receive your backups or your password.

5. Purchases

Steady Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

6. Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time: Settings › Profile › Delete Account and All Data (or Settings › Delete All Data if you use the app without an account). Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

7. Children

Steady Habit is not directed at children under 13.

8. Not medical advice

Steady Habit is a self-help tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. If you are struggling, please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
